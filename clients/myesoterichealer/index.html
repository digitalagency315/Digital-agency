The session

One unhurried session, in three parts

A Quantum Healing Hypnosis session may take around five hours. The intention is to look at your main concerns in depth, all within a single session.

I

Interview

1 to 2 hours

You and Marina talk through your main concerns in depth, so the session is shaped around what matters most to you.

II

Hypnosis

1 to 2 hours

About 1 hour
