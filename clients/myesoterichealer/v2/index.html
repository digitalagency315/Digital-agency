Is QHHT safe? Discover the benefits of Quantum Healing Hypnosis

Quantum Healing Hypnosis Technique is a hypnosis method developed by the late Dolores Cannon over her 40+ year career. It takes you into the deepest level of trance, allowing past life regression and healing through contact with the “Higher Self” or “Subconscious”.

This part of the self is loving, and ready to share insights into why we experience what we do in this lifetime, and how we might make sense of pain, stress and trauma. Many people ask: is QHHT safe? At My Esoteric Healer, your safety and personalised care come first. Located in the serene hinterland of Canungra, we offer a tranquil setting for your journey.

Your session with Marina

A Quantum Healing Hypnosis session may take around five hours.

The intention is to explore your main concerns in depth in a single session. The day is broken up into three parts: Interview, Hypnosis and Debrief. Your Higher Self will guide us to where we need to go to find the answers you seek.

011 to 2 hours

Interview

We will discuss your main concerns in depth. Getting to know you matters, so that I can ask relevant questions while you're under hypnosis.

021 to 2 hours

Hypnosis

We will ‘travel’ to an alternate time. This could be earlier in your current life, a past life, or even a future life. We then contact your Higher Self or Subconscious Mind to ask questions and receive healing.

031 hour

Debrief

You may or may not remember the session, so we take time to talk through what happened and let you return to your normal waking state before you go on with your day.

Before

Your questions

What you want to know directs where we go, and my job is to bring back relevant answers and guidance for you, so we use the time wisely. Please bring a list of questions: the serious ones you're desperate to have answered, and some ‘curiosity’ questions too. Don't limit yourself. Ask what's in your heart, and we'll go through them together.

During

Recording

The hypnosis part of the session is recorded on my laptop and given to you at the end. You can also record it on your own phone. Listening regularly lets the insights keep integrating after the session. Please don't listen while driving, as you can enter a Theta state.

After

Conclusion of session

As we wrap up, take a moment to enjoy the calm and clarity of the day. Take some time over the next few days to reflect on your experience and any new insights, and be gentle with yourself. Insights often keep unfolding after a session.
